01 / THE LITTLE PUSH

Motion leaves a trace.

The globe follows your hand on a damped spring. As it accelerates, the particles lag behind and the water-like current begins to swirl. Use the shake button for the same experiment with a keyboard or a tap.

02 / THE IN-BETWEEN

A beautifully slow fall.

Particles follow a swirling flow, lose energy and gradually settle under gravity. Change the settling speed to linger a little longer, or let the moment come to rest.

03 / BEHIND THE GLASS

Small world. Simple rules.

Thousands of particles share a simplified current field, bounded by the globe. This is a playful particle simulation, not a full fluid solver. The miniature worlds are crafted in Blender and rendered with Three.js.
